# Course Title: Prompt Engineering for Generative AI (2 hours)

## Course Description

Large language models can generate text, analyze information, and assist with daily work, but vague or unstructured prompts often produce generic, inaccurate, or inconsistent responses. Beginners frequently struggle to translate their intent into instructions that a model can reliably follow.

This course teaches a practical framework for writing clear, structured prompts that produce quality results. Students practice core prompting elements, few-shot examples, and iterative refinement through hands-on exercises in Google Gemini.

## Learning Objectives

- Write structured prompts that reliably produce accurate, high-quality results from generative AI models
- Explain how large language models interpret instructions and assemble the core components of an effective prompt
- Guide model outputs and formatting using zero-shot, few-shot, and step-by-step prompting techniques
- Refine, troubleshoot, and verify prompts through systematic iteration while avoiding common pitfalls

## Who Should Attend

Beginners, business professionals, and everyday users who want to get reliable, high-quality results from generative AI tools.

## Prerequisites

- Basic familiarity with generative AI chat interfaces
- Access to a Google Gemini account

## Hands-On Activities

- Lab: Writing Structured Prompts in Gemini
- Lab: Applying Few-Shot and Step-by-Step Prompting
- Lab: Iterating and Troubleshooting Prompts

## Course Outline

**Module 1: Foundations of Prompt Engineering**

- How Large Language Models Interpret Prompts
- The Anatomy of an Effective Prompt (Persona, Task, Context, Constraints)
- Specifying Output Format and Tone
- Lab: Writing Structured Prompts in Gemini

**Module 2: Core Prompting Techniques**

- Zero-Shot vs. Few-Shot Prompting
- Guiding Behavior with Examples
- Step-by-Step Reasoning and Task Decomposition
- Lab: Applying Few-Shot and Step-by-Step Prompting

**Module 3: Iterative Refinement and Best Practices**

- Iterative Prompting Workflows
- Managing Hallucinations and Verifying Outputs
- Common Prompting Pitfalls and Security Awareness
- Lab: Iterating and Troubleshooting Prompts
